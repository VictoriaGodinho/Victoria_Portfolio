“The Hidden Cost of Engagement”

Project Demo

▶ View Live Project

Description

The Instagram Ads Project is an interactive web experience that exposes the hidden economy behind user interactions with Instagram ads. Even the smallest gestures—pausing, scrolling, clicking— are recorded as signals of interest, generating revenue for advertisers and Instagram. This project simulates how much money flows from these invisible interactions, turning user behavior into measurable value.

Observations

While building the project, I discovered how even unconscious actions on social media platforms are turned into profit. Every scroll, pause, and click becomes measurable data that fuels the ad economy. In my simulation, if a user clicks on an ad, an additional $3.00 is added to the total; if they simply view or hover over it, $0.50 is automatically counted. These numbers are not exact real-world values but symbolic estimates meant to visualize the hidden flow of money behind user engagement.

The ads featured in the project were taken directly from my own Instagram data download, which made the experience feel more personal and grounded. By using my actual ad interactions, the project connects digital behavior with real traces of data that platforms store and monetize. Rather than critiquing or blaming the system, my goal was to spark curiosity and awareness; to help users notice how their attention itself can generate value, even without making a purchase. The experience turns an everyday, invisible process into something tangible and interactive, encouraging reflection on the unseen exchanges that shape our online environments.
